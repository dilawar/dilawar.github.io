- 🔐I was curious if anyone is using Probably Approximately Correct Learning (PAC Learning, [PAC: Valiant84.pdf](../../../undefined) ) in cyber-security. Its not yet mainstream but some folks have been proposing it as an alternative to classical $\epsilon \delta$-differential privacy [[2210.03458] PAC Privacy: Automatic Privacy Measurement and Control of Data Processing](../../../undefined) (summary in [Trip report: ‘CWI Lectures on Secure Computation’ (Amsterdam, NL) | Roseman Labs Tech Blog](../../../../Home/377628b0b08a4a54929984c7d620d657/References/Trip%20report%20%E2%80%98CWI%20Lectures%20on%20Secure%20Computation%E2%80%99%20(%202c1e579bff89811681ccfab802261bdd.md)). I liked it! PAC gives you lower bounds for free so in many situation, you don’t have to assume or compute  the worst-case scenario youself which are great for proving guarantees.
