- We developers are often thinking about software architecture from the point of view of development so much that we forget to consider operational issues like “blast radius” e.g. what happens when a component of system is going through failure e.g. an email service stops not working because AWS blacklisted your SES keys?!
    - If you are a small team, ensure that someone is thinking seriously about these issue during design phase.
