- Which is a better input to an AI, text or pixels? Many believe pixels to be better https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45658928. Karpathy worked (works?) at Tesla which has its driving system entirely based on camera inputs!
    - I find pixels to be more “natural” input in biological systems than languages e.g. mammalian vision systems. Olfaction and haptics are equally natural. All of these modalities looks very different from each other to me even though they create final representation in neural activity. A bit like how Haskell and TeX are different even though they run on the same processor!!
    - I don’t think there is something terribly common in these two modalities even if there are correlates/similarities in neural representation? Perhaps vision is much more complex than other modalities and [More Is Different | Science](../../../undefined).
