- I am trying to set up engineering workflow at Dognosis (my current employer). After brainstorming with ChatGPT, I have a template [**A Lightweight Execution Workflow for Small Engineering Teams**](../../../A%20Notion%20Home/0.%20Public%20Website/Blog/@Last%20Saturday%20Weekly%20Notes%202026%2002%20%281%29/A%20Lightweight%20Execution%20Workflow%20for%20Small%20Enginee.md).
    - I spelled out what I am looking for in the prompt and what I don’t want.
        - First thing in the week is a short meeting and everyone should know they are going to do the rest of the week independently.
        - Every Friday, we do the review, update status and report it to the stakeholders.
        - During the week, no cat herding! Each engineer acts like a professional and in turn treated like one (I don’t ask for update).
        - Write before talking. Meeting must have written agenda.
