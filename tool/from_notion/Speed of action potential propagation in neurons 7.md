# Speed of action potential propagation in neurons

Published On: April 28, 2014
Author: dilawar
Status: Published
Type: Note
Tags: note

Same as motorized vehicle: 30 kmph (scooters, unmyelinated neuron) to 500 kmph (F1 cars, myelinated neurons). 

If you think about it, it makes perfect sense that our ability to handle vehicles that are driven by us is in the same range as speed of action potential. We can’t react faster than the information flow.
